╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                       GETTING STARTED                                ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
• Right-click on the desktop to access the Openbox menu items.
• Right-click on the desktop and select 'Exit' to log out. 
• Right-click → Next Login - Switch between two desktop layouts.
  - Desktop layout A : Traditional panel
  - Desktop layout B : Dock and launcher

To switch desktop layouts, right-click on the desktop, choose
'Next Login', and select your preferred layout.
The selected layout becomes active after your next login.

To change icons and GTK themes, launch "Appearance Theme" from the
application menu.

Enjoy Openbox Workstation!
